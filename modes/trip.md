# Trip

1. Read the profile, `modes/flights.md` and `modes/stays.md`. Identify the trip's
   destinations, outbound/return dates, party and preferences; ask once for
   missing facts. Explain the scope before expanding routes or dates.
2. Search flights first using the flights workflow. Let the human choose the
   itinerary; account for arrival/departure local dates and overnight travel.
3. Search stays for the chosen itinerary's actual destination arrival date and
   return departure date. Use the approved place and stays party. Do not assume
   a hotel night on the aircraft or a stay length from UTC calendar dates.
4. Present separate flight and stay cards, plus a combined total only from
   compatible returned currencies or dated conversions. Label flight vs stay
   party differences and unknown/excluded costs. No fabricated budget estimates.
5. End with every source report and all scope/coverage/filter notes from both
   searches. A failure in one part does not erase results from the other.
