# 12Go

## Access

12Go publishes an MCP server at `https://mcp.12go.asia/mcp`: no key, no account.
One tool, `search`. A session handshake goes first, on a short line of its own.

## Request

`from`, `to` (English place names), `date`, `seats` (adults), `fxcode: EUR`,
`lang: en`, `v: all`, `sort: Cheapest`. The server defaults are two seats and Thai
baht, so both are always sent.

## Answer

A list of trips: `from` and `to` station names, `price`, `departure` and `arrival`
as local wall-clock times without a zone, `vehclasses` (bus, train, ferry, van,
avia) and `booking_url`, 12Go's page of that route and day.

## Prices and limits

- `price` is one seat: Belgrade to Sarajevo cost 30 EUR at one seat and at two
  (2026-10-07). The adapter multiplies by the adults. Children's fares are not
  shown, so a party with children is not configured.
- No carrier names and no time zones: a ride has no duration when it crosses one.
- One bus is sold to every stop on its way: Belgrade to Sarajevo also listed the
  same 07:00 bus to Ustiprača. A ride to a stop short of the place is left out when
  the same departure also reaches it, and the note names the stops.
- Flights in the answer are not kept: the flight sources cover them.
- A short answer: a few trips per route, the cheapest by 12Go's own order.

## Verified, 2026-10-07

Belgrade to Sarajevo, 14 November: two buses, 30 and 35 EUR a seat. Belgrade to
Vienna: seven buses from 38 EUR a seat. Both from a home address.
